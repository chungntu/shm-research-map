Programme synthesis · 20 November 2026 · 51 projects across 17 research tracks plus 7 semantic cross-track labels (Virtual Sensing, One-Class, Physics-ML, Sensor Loss, Rail, HDPE, Wind)

The programme asks how structural health monitoring and ocean infrastructure engineering can recover unmeasured responses, detect and localize damage, and remain resilient when sensors fail or harsh operating conditions intervene. Every paper keeps a unique primary code tied to its physical folder, while cross-cutting studies carry up to two optional semantic labels stacked vertically below the primary code (at most 3 labels total per paper) without renaming folders or consuming sequence counters.

1. Virtual sensing with limited measurements · VS1–VS4, VS8–VS12, VS15 (Wind), VS18–VS26 · linked VS13–VS14 (HDPE)
VS1 (Rail) and VS2 (Rail) estimate quasi-static stress and deck strain on KW51, while VS12 (Rail) tests conformal coverage across retrofit and operational shifts. VS3 asks which lost signals remain recoverable, VS4 studies when sensors should sample under energy constraints, and VS8 relates sensor budget to spectral rank. On Hardanger, VS9 uses dynamic structural condensation, VS11 studies block-Hankel completion and recovery certificates for fixed outage patterns, and VS18 (formerly GN2) asks when a masked-node graph network learns the condensation of VS9. VS10 develops modal graph sampling in space and time. In wind and marine energy, VS15 (Wind) extends physics-guided virtual sensing to 15 MW floating wind turbine moorings, VS19 (Wind · pilot GO) validates safety-gated catenary–ML mooring sensing on measured wave-tank data (Blue Growth Farm), VS20 (Wind · conditional GO) investigates subsea monopile strain estimation on WILLOW Norther, VS21 (Wind · complete, target Renewable Energy) builds a virtual nacelle anemometer via a power-driven Gaussian wake model on Kelmarsh and Penmanshiel, and VS26 explores modal–ML riser VIV fatigue reconstruction. Extending hybrid virtual sensing to further civil domains, VS22 targets post-earthquake building interstory drift (CESMD), VS23 transfers 1D heat-equation pavement depth temperature from LTPP to Vietnam (following PV1), VS24 studies damage-robust truss strain expansion on Hell Bridge, and VS25 tests moving-train mode superposition on Nam Ô Bridge. VS13 (HDPE) and VS17 (HDPE) apply virtual sensing and inverse digital twins to marine fish cages under the dedicated HDPE cluster below.
2. Damage detection, localization and sensor faults · OC1–OC4, TL1–TL5, FT1, FT3, CW1, GN1
OC1–OC3 develop one-class representations on Z24 and AQUINAS. OC4 examines the trade-off between temperature robustness and damage sensitivity on the Sonkyo blade, while CW1 conditions a WaveNet detector on temperature. TL1–TL3 use HBTA topology for edge transfer, sensor restoration and connection localization. TL4 uses transverse modes on Route 345, and TL5 shows on LUMO Tower that the evaluation design (reference state after interventions, calibration window, validation split) decides reported detection and localization performance more than the choice of algorithm. FT1 uses the wind–traffic crossover to condition one-class reconstruction on Hardanger, while FT3 studies persistent sensor quarantine and detector reconfiguration on HBTA. GN1 detects performance changes of a wind-farm turbine from its neighbours, without a hand-picked reference turbine.
3. Transfer across structures and datasets · HP1, PS1
HP1 compares the contribution of input formulation and hyperparameter optimization in supervised damage classification. PS1 studies when knowledge transfer helps across monitored structures and when differences in topology or measurement distributions lead to negative transfer.
4. Structural dynamics, stability and field identification · WT1, EL1–EL2, ST1–ST2, WL1
WT1 tests consistency across measurement setups in offshore wind foundation modal identification. EL1 studies two-sided bounds for equivalent linearization, and EL2 examines dissipation and dephasing in measured damping. ST1 investigates when the end portal governs out-of-plane stability on KW51 and Nam Ô. ST2 studies how hanger tension changes lateral restraint and what that means for updating the KW51 model. WL1 asks what measured accelerations of the Hardanger Bridge reveal about the modal distribution of the wind load.
5. Vision-based inspection and measurement · CV1–CV4
CV1 uses vision foundation models to detect and segment bridge surface damage. CV2 develops a semi-automatic workflow for labeling multiple damage classes. CV3 uses camera-based, non-contact vibration measurement to extend vision from surface inspection to structural response. CV4 co-designs a non-intrusive high-speed railway inspection pod integrating laser-Doppler synchronization, inertial-anchor rectification, and hybrid rail-defect detection.
6. Pavement engineering, climate resilience & green transportation · PV1
PV1 establishes the first comprehensive 34-year (1991–2024) nationwide analysis of Weighted Mean Annual Pavement Temperature (WMAPT) across 127 monitoring stations in Vietnam using LTPP InfoPave reanalysis data. It identifies a +0.83°C warming trend, quantifies the spatial divergence in pavement design temperatures between coastal and inland zones (Khánh Hòa case study), and proposes climate-resilient asphalt pavement design guidelines to mitigate rutting and fatigue failures under rising temperatures.
7. Offshore wind and mooring virtual sensing · VS15 (Wind)
VS15 (linked to the Wind cluster) estimates mooring-line tensions of a 15 MW floating wind turbine from topside measurements only and detects and identifies line failures without damaged training data. A closed-form catenary compliance proves the span–tension map invertible, a catenary prior with a learned dynamic residual (PGRE) stays accurate outside the training distribution, and a force-envelope detector found all four line failures in authentic OpenFAST data with no false alarm. The manuscript and submission dossier are complete; submission to Ocean Engineering is planned.
8. Multi-cage mooring-system design · MO1 (HDPE)
MO1 (grouped into the HDPE aquaculture cluster) is a multi-objective design study of shared-grid mooring systems for multi-cage HDPE fish-farm arrays, with progressive-collapse prevention under extreme storm conditions ($21.5\%$ mooring CapEx saving while maintaining $SF_{\text{damaged}} \ge 1.40$). The 15-page manuscript draft is compiled and under refinement for Marine Structures.
9. Ship roll early warning · MS1
MS1 uses physics-guided neural ODEs for real-time early warning of parametric roll resonance in container ships. The manuscript is complete and compiled; journal submission is pending.
10. Aquaculture structures & HDPE marine fish cages · HDPE (VS13, VS14, VS17, MO1)
The semantic HDPE group unites the marine fish-cage engineering papers into one coherent programme without changing primary repository names: VS13 (HDPE) establishes analytical observability and virtual sensing of bending moments along floating HDPE collars from sparse accelerometers; VS14 (HDPE) develops a multi-fidelity Gaussian-process surrogate and NSGA-II storm design framework for dual-ring HDPE cages under typhoon conditions; VS17 (HDPE) develops a time-dependent digital twin that inversely identifies net biofouling thickness from surface drift for predictive net-cleaning schedules; and MO1 (HDPE) extends optimization and progressive-collapse safety to shared-grid multi-cage HDPE fish-farm moorings.
11. Composite damage diagnosis · CS1
CS1 explores vibration-based delamination diagnosis in CFRP plates using accelerometers and one-class AI; it remains an early-stage research idea.

Connections between studies & semantic clusters

  • KW51 & Railway corridor (Rail · VS1, VS2, VS12, VS24, VS25, ST1, ST2, CV4): VS1 and VS2 estimate stresses and strains on KW51, VS12 checks conformal intervals across retrofit shifts, VS24 and VS25 extend hybrid virtual sensing to Hell Bridge and Nam Ô railway trusses, ST1–ST2 quantify tied-arch out-of-plane buckling and hanger tension bracing on KW51 and Nam Ô, and CV4 designs a non-intrusive high-speed track inspection pod.
  • Hardanger: VS3, VS9 and VS11 examine recoverability and reconstruction under sensor loss, while VS18 (formerly GN2) learns structural condensation using masked-node graph networks. FT1 addresses the downstream task of damage detection.
  • HBTA: TL2 ranks sensors for restoration, FT3 studies how the detector should operate during persistent faults, TL3 uses the remaining modal information to localize connection damage, and VS24 explores damage-robust virtual strain sensing on the same truss.
  • Wind energy (Wind · VS15, VS19, VS20, VS21, WT1, OC4, GN1): WT1 audits offshore turbine foundation setup consistency, OC4 probes blade thermal-damage sensitivity, GN1 calibrates turbine upgrade gains, VS15 and VS19 monitor floating wind/multi-purpose moorings (OpenFAST + Blue Growth Farm wave tank), VS20 estimates subsea monopile strain on WILLOW Norther, and VS21 reconstructs missing nacelle wind speeds via a power-driven wake model on the companion Kelmarsh and Penmanshiel SCADA datasets.
  • Aquaculture & HDPE fish cages (HDPE · VS13, VS14, VS17, MO1): VS13 monitors collar bending, VS14 optimizes dual-ring storm sizing, VS17 tracks time-varying net biofouling drag, and MO1 protects multi-cage array moorings against progressive collapse.
